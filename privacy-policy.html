Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Indoone AI ("Indoone", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Indoone AI application, our WhatsApp messaging integration, and related services. We use information only as reasonably necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and improve these services.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and profile information: details you provide when creating or managing an Indoone account, such as account identifiers, email address, mobile number, and profile fields.
  • Messages and AI interactions: the text you send to Indoone, the prompts and replies needed to provide the service, and related conversation metadata.
  • WhatsApp information: when you message our WhatsApp business number, we may receive your phone number or WhatsApp user identifier, message text, timestamps, and delivery or event metadata provided through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Technical and security data: limited request, diagnostic, and error information needed to keep the service reliable, prevent abuse, investigate failures, and protect security.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to your messages and requests, operate AI and chat features, manage accounts and preferences, maintain security, diagnose technical problems, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Depending on the feature and request, a query may be processed by relevant search or research sources to provide requested information.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

Messages sent through WhatsApp are processed through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and our backend so we can receive messages and send replies. Meta processes information under its own terms and policies. We use WhatsApp message data to provide the messaging service, maintain its security, and troubleshoot delivery or processing issues. Automated replies may be generated by Indoone AI.

4. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may process or disclose information to service providers that support our services, including infrastructure hosting, authentication and data storage, messaging delivery, and search or research functions when used. We may also disclose information where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our legal rights. Service providers process information for their relevant service purposes.

5. Storage and retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining account and conversation functionality, security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods can differ by information type and feature.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including access controls and protected handling of service credentials. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and data deletion requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we control by emailing indoone@zohomail.in with the subject Privacy Request. Please describe the account or WhatsApp interaction concerned and the action you are requesting. We may need to verify your request before acting. We will respond and handle valid requests as required by applicable law; some information may need to be retained for security or legal reasons.

8. Children's privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a way that should not have occurred, please contact us at indoone@zohomail.in so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
